# FireLearn source and content policy

FireLearn is an educational study aid. It is not a certification authority, operational SOP, AHJ directive, or replacement for supervised practical training.

## Source hierarchy

1. Local department SOPs/SOGs, AHJ requirements, and incident command direction.
2. Instructor-led certification materials and practical skill sheets.
3. Adopted standards and training references such as NFPA 1001, NFPA 470/472 lineage, ERG guidance for hazmat awareness, and locally adopted fire code material.
4. FireLearn summaries, quizzes, videos, and flashcards.

If a FireLearn lesson conflicts with local SOPs, AHJ direction, or instructor guidance, the local/official source wins.

## Content rules for future expansion

- Every rich lesson must include references and operational boundaries.
- Fireground tactics should be framed as study and recognition patterns, not authorization to act.
- Hazmat, mayday, rescue, ventilation, structural collapse, and medical topics require explicit safety disclaimers.
- Video tutorials must be treated as supplemental media, not primary authority.
- Course certificates are study records only and must not imply formal certification.

## Current implementation

- Lesson pages display per-lesson references.
- Footer displays the global educational disclaimer.
- Certificates include the same disclaimer.
- Progress, streaks, and completion data are learning signals only.
